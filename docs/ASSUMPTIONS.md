# Product decisions

- Each browser origin/profile is an independent, device-local shop. There is no backend, sync, telemetry or remote runtime asset. Login is memory-only and reload requires login again.
- Seed two PS4 and two PS5 rooms, tea at EGP 10, coffee at EGP 15, and the specified four expense categories. All are editable. Decreasing counts deactivates historical rooms, removes unused rooms, and refuses busy rooms.
- The visual design uses dark navy, lime accents and coral busy states. The theme is consistently dark. The placeholder controller icons are generated locally with maskable safe space. Cairo regular is bundled under OFL; browsers synthesize bold.
- Cairo is the default configurable timezone. Sessions snapshot their rate, timezone and rounding policy so settings changes do not reprice existing sessions. Room/service names and service prices are also snapshotted.
- The confirmed thresholds are 35 and 45 in defaults and session pricing. **49 minutes bills 60, not 45.** The explicit table supersedes the conflicting original example. Zero minutes bills 30 with rounding on, zero with it off. Elapsed minutes are floor(milliseconds / 60,000).
- Currency inputs use Western digits and at most two decimals, parsed into integer piasters. Percentage inputs use basis points: 10.25% is 1025. Percentage discounts round fractional piasters upward and cap at subtotal. Exact-price mode also rounds fractional piasters upward. Other price modes round the play charge upward to 50 or 100 piasters, once per session. Conversion to fractional pounds happens only for display/export.
- Fixed discounts cap at subtotal; percentages above 100% are rejected. Zero service prices and zero bills are allowed. Expenses must be positive. Negative amounts are rejected.
- **Gross play revenue** = finished sessions' play charges. **Gross services revenue** = their service charges. **Discounts** = their discounts. **Net = gross play + gross services - discounts - nonvoided expenses.** Discounts are subtracted once. **Unpaid = sum(max(total - paid, 0))**; revenue counts the full bill. Change is max(paid - total, 0).
- Running sessions show provisional accrual but are excluded from finalized totals, services sold and finished counts. Cancelled/voided sessions contribute zero and appear separately. Finished counts include underpaid sessions, with unpaid shown separately. Room ranges include audit records for traceability.
- Closing is blocked until all running sessions are ended or cancelled; the dialog opens each session to do either. Closed shifts are immutable, including expenses and sessions. Thus voiding a finished session is permitted only during its open shift. There is no reopening. Closing snapshots settings, logo, user names, sessions, expenses and totals. Day reports include every shift's totals plus full detail.
- A shift belongs to its start date in the configured timezone, even across midnight; later timezone changes do not regroup it. The datetime input uses the device's native timezone. Reports use stored timezone names.
- Start time cannot be future or before shift opening. Native inputs have minute precision; leaving the default unchanged starts at the actual confirmation timestamp. Explicit edits use the entered minute and are rejected if before shift opening. Editing a running session requires editSessionTime. Checkout captures its end timestamp on opening payment, so typing does not increase the bill; reopening checkout captures a new end.
- No server clock correction exists. Backward clock jumps clamp provisional displays to zero; ending before start is rejected. Forward jumps increase the bill. Fix the device clock or use authorized time editing. Wall-clock idle lock can also be affected by clock adjustments.
- Idle lock defaults off (0). When configured it includes time away from the app and watches pointer/key activity. Reload and logout require login again without stopping timers.
- Admin alone manages users, recovery, audit visibility and restore. Nonadmins with manageBackup may export, but may not replace security data via restore. Editing service lines uses startSession; end and discount permissions are independent. Admin cannot be demoted and the active admin cannot deactivate itself. New users default to start/end permissions.
- Recovery codes contain 96 random bits in grouped hexadecimal. Comparison ignores surrounding whitespace and letter case. Store a separate salted PBKDF2 hash, show the code once until acknowledged, and retain validity until replaced. Password/recovery reset is audited.
- ExcelJS is loaded from a precached local chunk. Exported money is numeric pounds, with decimal formats and RTL worksheets. Browser printing provides real Arabic shaping and A4 landscape for wide tables. Sections break pages and closing reports include a signature line. The document title suggests the requested PDF filename; the browser controls the final save dialog/name.
- Restore is explicit whole-device replacement after summary and admin confirmation. A safety backup is offered, not forced. First-run restore requires no prior account. JSON is unencrypted and includes hashes/history. Download messages describe the usual folder without claiming to know a full path. Drive uploads are manual.
- The dismissible backup reminder is limited to once per current local date after shift close. History/audit records are never pruned.
- Writes reread the authoritative snapshot in an IndexedDB read/write transaction, protecting concurrent financial writes and busy-room invariants. Settings edits are explicitly saved and the last saved edit wins. One operator tab is recommended.
- Persistent storage is requested on setup; denial does not block use. Errors do not commit success state. Logo input is capped at 5 MB and resized to at most 512px; restore input is capped at 100 MB.
- GitHub returned HTTP 422 enabling Pages: the current plan does not support this private repository. Keep it private. CI validation/build artifacts work; deployment is gated by PAGES_ENABLED=true after Pages becomes available. No hosted URL is claimed.
